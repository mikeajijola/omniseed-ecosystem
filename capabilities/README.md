# Company capability reference catalogue

Version 1.0.0. This governance-owned catalogue is reference knowledge, not canonical company desired state. Companies may record selected IDs as provenance while declaring and specialising their own capabilities in canonical Omniform; this repository is never a runtime dependency.

## strategy governance

- **govern_strategy — Govern strategy:** Direction, priorities, decisions, and accountability remain explicit.

## product service

- **manage_products_services — Manage products and services:** Offerings are discovered, shaped, delivered, and retired against customer outcomes.

## research innovation

- **conduct_research_innovation — Conduct research and innovation:** Uncertainty is reduced through evidence-generating research and experiments.

## engineering

- **engineer_products — Engineer products:** Product changes are designed, verified, released, and maintained.

## platform it

- **operate_platforms — Operate platforms and IT:** Digital services remain available, supportable, and recoverable.

## security risk privacy

- **secure_company — Secure company operations:** Security, privacy, compliance, and enterprise risks are identified and controlled.

## finance accounting

- **manage_finance — Manage finance and accounting:** Cash, accounts, forecasts, obligations, and financial decisions are controlled.

## legal corporate

- **administer_legal_corporate — Administer legal and corporate matters:** Legal obligations, entities, contracts, and governance records are maintained.

## people learning

- **develop_people — Develop people and organisation:** The company attracts, supports, develops, and retains suitable contributors.

## sales revenue

- **grow_revenue — Grow revenue:** Qualified demand becomes governed, durable customer revenue.

## marketing communications

- **communicate_market — Communicate with the market:** Audiences can discover, understand, and trust the company and its offerings.

## partnerships community

- **cultivate_ecosystem — Cultivate partnerships and community:** External relationships create mutual, governed value.

## customer success support

- **support_customers — Onboard and support customers:** Customers adopt offerings, obtain help, and achieve intended outcomes.

## procurement vendors

- **manage_suppliers — Manage suppliers:** External goods and services are selected, governed, and reviewed.

## data analytics knowledge

- **manage_data_knowledge — Manage data and knowledge:** Trusted information is findable, interpretable, retained, and used responsibly.

## incident continuity

- **assure_continuity — Assure incident response and continuity:** The company detects disruption, responds, recovers, and learns.

## accelerator programmes

- **operate_programmes — Operate programmes and accelerators:** Cohorts and programmes are selected, delivered, evaluated, and improved.
