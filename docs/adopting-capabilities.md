# Adopting catalogue capabilities

The catalogue is discovery material, not imported desired state. A company selects only relevant entries, copies the stable catalogue ID into its change rationale or provenance, and declares its own capability plus primitive-family requirements in canonical Omniform. The company may narrow the outcome to its context, but must not reuse an ID for a contradictory outcome.

For example, a software company may adopt `engineer_products`, `operate_platforms`, `secure_company`, and `support_customers`; a community accelerator may adopt `operate_programmes`, `cultivate_ecosystem`, `develop_people`, and `manage_finance`. In both cases the company chooses its own actors, Providers, policies, realisations, and evidence expectations.

Contributions require a durable ID, an outcome distinct from existing entries, actor/Provider-neutral requirements, and semantic review for overlaps. A rename that preserves meaning retains the ID; a meaning-breaking change creates a new ID and deprecates the old entry in a future schema revision.
